Getting around

Teshikaga is a large rural area. Rental cars are a convenient way to explore the lakes, viewpoints and hot spring areas. Bus services are available on selected routes.

Nature & seasons

Enjoy the changing landscapes throughout the year, from clear summer lakes and autumn colors to snowy winter scenery. Weather and road conditions can change quickly.

Travel responsibly

Please respect wildlife, stay on designated trails, follow local rules and take your rubbish with you. Some natural areas have restricted access.
